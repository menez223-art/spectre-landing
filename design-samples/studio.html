Enter your email and your sheet is created automatically — it appears in your Drive shared with you, ready to receive orders. You can create more than one sheet (each email gets an independent sheet).

  1. Open Google Sheets and create a new spreadsheet.
  2. From the top menu: Extensions then Apps Script.
  3. Clear the existing code and paste the required Apps Script code.
  4. Press Deploy then New deployment then Web app.
  5. Leave Execute as: Me, and change Who has access to Anyone.
  6. Press Deploy then copy the /exec link that appears — paste it into the field below and save.

Orders from every page you generate go straight to this sheet — don't share the link.

Create a Meta Pixel step by step No code needed — just two minutes
  1. Open business.facebook.com and sign in with your normal Facebook account.
  2. From the tools menu choose «Events Manager».
  3. Click «Connect a Data Source» then «Web» then «Get Started».
  4. Name it after your store, then copy the number shown next to it (about 15 digits).
  5. Paste that number in the Pixel field here, press Save, then republish your page to activate it.
