How to review

  • Theme: moon/sun button (top corner) switches light/dark instantly.
  • Language: AR/EN button swaps every text and flips direction RTL/LTR.
  • Review bar (bottom, dashed): switches page states and opens modals. Press − to collapse it so it never covers content.
  • Score each page (out of 10) and add notes; real-code application waits for your explicit acceptance.
Samples are local-only (offline). External links are inert (#), store/admin data is labeled demo data, and the studio live-preview is a labeled placeholder — published landing pages are out of scope by agreement. The accent-color picker (ThemeSelector) was dropped because the new identity has a fixed palette.
